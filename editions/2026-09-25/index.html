Research01

Communicating agents show conditional gains in discovery tasks

A new preprint tests agents that exchange information through a shared directory. The authors report better results than independent parallel attempts on several discovery tasks, including ARC-AGI-3 and classifier compression. They also find that communication can lose its advantage when compute is scarce or progress is hard to measure. These are benchmark results, not evidence that collaboration improves every deployment.

Why it matters: Evaluate communication overhead and task feedback before paying for larger agent teams.

Research02

Swarm scaling trades token efficiency for speed

Toby Ord’s analysis of published small-swarm results argues that adding agents can shorten turnaround while consuming more total tokens for comparable performance. The estimates depend on the task and orchestration method. His September 27 correction removes an earlier extrapolation about a much larger mathematics swarm, so that claim is not repeated here.

Why it matters: Separate wall-clock speed, total cost and capability when comparing multi-agent systems.
