OpenAI says its internal agents can carry out well-defined research tasks under human direction, including work that takes a skilled researcher days. Claude formalizes an existing proof of Fermat’s Last Theorem

Anthropic reports a complete Lean formalization produced largely autonomously over 11 days, with human high-level guidance. The work translates established mathematics into computer-checkable form; it is not a newly discovered proof of a previously unsolved conjecture. The company publishes the work and quotes mathematician Kevin Buzzard’s assessment.

Why it matters: Formal checking can reduce the verification burden as machine-generated mathematics expands.
